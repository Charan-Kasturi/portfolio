Who I Am

I'm a Robotics Engineer building intelligent autonomous systems at the intersection of AI, computer vision, and motion planning.

Currently completing my Master of Engineering in Robotics at the University of Maryland, College Park, I specialize in autonomous navigation, SLAM, and deep learning for real-world robotics. Originally from Hyderabad, India, I've crossed continents to pursue cutting-edge research.

My projects span fully autonomous robot development with ROS & C++, reinforcement learning for self-driving cars, and real-time computer vision using custom YOLO architectures. I thrive at the boundary of classical robotics and modern AI.

When I'm not building robots, I'm watching cricket — or playing it.
