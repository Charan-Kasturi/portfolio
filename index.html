01 · ABOUT

Who I Am

I'm a Robotics Engineer driven by the challenge of building machines that can operate intelligently in the real world.

Currently completing my Master of Engineering in Robotics at the University of Maryland, College Park, I specialise in autonomous navigation, SLAM, motion planning, and deep learning for perception. Originally from Hyderabad, India, I've channelled a lifelong curiosity for technology into building systems at the frontier of robotics and AI.

From building fully autonomous robots with ROS & C++, to training DQN agents to drive in simulation, to deploying real-time YOLO-based perception — I love the full stack of robotics engineering.

Outside the lab, you'll find me following cricket with an unhealthy level of enthusiasm.
